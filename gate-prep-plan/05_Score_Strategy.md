## N.3 Preparing for both simultaneously — the strategy

1. **One syllabus, two exams:** ~50% of each paper is shared (Section D). The order in Phase 1–2 guarantees the shared spine is done once, at the deeper (DA) level.
2. **CS is the depth paper; DA is the leverage paper:** CSE-only content (six subjects, ~45 marks) needs 60% of your hours; DA-only content (ML/AI/stats/warehousing, ~30 marks) needs ~25%; the rest is shared study counted once.
3. **Two full-length mock tracks from January** (CS series + DA series) — never merge them; the papers reward different rhythms (CS: systems reasoning; DA: math precision).
4. **Rank math:** your "second" paper costs only ~20% extra total hours for a full extra rank/score. This is the single highest-leverage decision you've made — both combinations are officially allowed in GATE 2027 (verify your chosen primary/secondary pair in the brochure Table 4 during registration).

# O. Final 30-Day Strategy (1 Feb → exams, 6–21 Feb 2027)

**Why this shape:** memory consolidation + exam simulation beat new content; the danger list (double-failed mistakes) predicts your actual losses better than any new topic will.

## O.1 The alternating-day engine (both papers stay warm)

- **Day A (CS day):** Full CS mock (or CS sectional ×2 in W17 if mocks are exhausted) + 3 h post-mortem + CS formula deck.
- **Day B (DA day):** Full DA mock / DA sectional ×2 + post-mortem + DA formula deck.
- **Daily, every day regardless:** 45 min = danger-list re-solves + 10 GA questions.
- Light days (day before each mock): formula deck full run + starred PYQ skim, no new content.

## O.2 Week-by-week

| Window | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1–6 Feb | CS mocks ×2, DA mocks ×2; finish formula decks; GA alternate-day full 15-mark sets |
| 7–13 Feb | CS exam likely in this window (per official dates 6/7, 13/14): taper CS 2 days out (O.3), keep DA light after the CS exam |
| 14–21 Feb | DA taper + exam; between-exam days: only formula decks + danger list |

## O.3 Last 2 days before any paper

1. Formula decks (both papers') — 90 min each.
2. Danger list — re-solve every double-failed question.
3. One light sectional (GA + your strongest subject) to stay warm — no full mock.
4. Logistics: admit card, ID, centre route, report 60 min early; sleep 7+ h.
5. Nothing new after the second-last day. New C1-level learning in the last 48 h hurts more than it helps.

## O.4 Exam-day execution (3 hours, both papers)

- **Order:** GA first (15 min, bank 13–14 marks) → your strongest subjects → mid subjects → hard/unknown last.
- **Time caps:** 1-mark ≤ 2 min; 2-mark ≤ 4 min on first pass; park and return.
- **Marking discipline:** MCQ — answer if you can eliminate ≥2 options or are ≥60% sure; MSQ/NAT — always make your best attempt (no negative).
- **At the 1-hour and 2-hour marks:** check parked questions; never leave NAT blank at the end — a 30-second best guess costs nothing.
- **DA-specific:** the paper is math-heavy; show intermediate values on the virtual notepad to protect against C2 errors; watch χ²/CI conventions.
- Target checkpoints: CS — leave the hall having attempted ~62–65 Qs; DA — ~58–62 Qs, with P&S/LA fully attempted.

## O.5 Post-exam
Between the two papers: half-day rest, then only the other paper's formula deck + 20 starred PYQs. Do not post-mortem the finished paper online — it costs the next paper's focus.
